Ontario Gas Pipe Sizing Calculator

Natural gas pipe sizing by the longest-length method — Schedule 40, 0.60 SG, 0.5 and 3.0 in. w.c. pressure-drop tables.

Enter load and length.

Method and basis

Longest-length method on Schedule 40 metallic pipe, natural gas at 0.60 specific gravity: round the longest run up to the next table length, then take the smallest pipe whose capacity at that length carries the total connected load. Capacities are in CFH; at 1,000 Btu/ft³, CFH numerically equals MBH. The CSA B149.1 Annex A tables sit behind the CSA paywall — these grids are the standard North American tables on the same basis, cross-checked across several manufacturer reproductions; verify against the current B149.1 Annex A before construction. A propane table is deliberately omitted: published reproductions conflict on its units.
